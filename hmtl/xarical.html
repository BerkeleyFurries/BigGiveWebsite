Xarical

The best method to contact me is through Discord, though email also will work. I can draw animal characters (specialization in dragons) and furries/anthros. I can try other things, but quality isn't guaranteed in that case. Things in particular that I will struggle with or in some cases cannot draw are humans/humanoids, complex armor/mecha designs, and complex backgrounds (cities, architecture, or anything that focuses on background details rather than characters). You may use this art for any purpose as long as credit is given. Completion time may vary depending on difficulty. I will try to keep you up to date on my availability to work on the artwork. I may use the artwork as an example for my portfolios. Do inform me if you wish to keep the artwork private. You are allowed to request work in progresses or updates at any time, however I may not be able to respond immediately if I am busy. I will try to provide a WIP whenever one stage is completed. I prefer having as much details provided as possible, it helps me create an artwork that is more accurate to what you are looking for! Example of details you can provide: A flat colored reference image / detailed appearance description of your character, the expressions and poses, background patterns, color theme, outfits.
