Hi!! I'm Cheddart and I love drawing furries! I'm the artist behind the Furries at Berkeley t-shirt and I also co-run a small art shop called Gobworm. Please contact me at cheddart@proton.me with any questions, especially if you have something in mind that I don't have a listed tier for, I really enjoy making art for people! TOS: - For all commissions you will receive a sketch to approve - any light revisions are okay at the sketch stage, any later revisions come at extra charge - No NSFW, extremely detailed backgrounds, NFTs, AI use - I reserve the right to deny a commission if I am uncomfortable with it - Completion time is ~4 weeks - Please credit me if you use my art anywhere on the internet. I reserve the right to use the artwork for my portfolio and on my social media. - Standard pricing is for personal use only, if you want to use it on anything you plan to sell (or any way commercially) pricing will go up.
