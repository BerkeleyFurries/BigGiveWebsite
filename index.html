What's the Big Give?

Have u heard of the Big Give? :D It's Berkeley's annual fundraiser for clubs and other orgs! This year the Big Give is Mar 11th - Mar 12th!!! FaB is participating, offering furry art as a reward for donating, drawn by our very own artists!
Take a looksie around to see all the wonderful artists you can donate to and receive art from! All proceeds go directly to the artists! Here you can donate and redeem art from the artists all in one place!
The Big Give offers bonus rewards for surpassing certain milestones like most individual donors. Donate on Mar 11th 9-10pm for the chance for our club to win an extra $7000 from the Big Give!!! This will help fund future club events and even exclusive club mascot plushies (Nelli Plush!!!!!!!)
